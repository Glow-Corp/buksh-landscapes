What is covered under your warranty?

Problems are uncommon, but if something related to workmanship comes up, we handle it quickly. That includes movement caused by defects in workmanship, such as improperly installed pavers, retaining walls or steps. Hardscape work has two years of coverage from completion. Turf and plant material are covered for one year from installation.

Normal settlement of newly placed fill, backfilled foundations or utility trenches, frost movement, and other ground movement outside our control aren't covered. Neither are issues caused by poor maintenance, pests, disease, or not following care instructions. Grass may not thrive in deep shade, near walls or in high-traffic spots.

How do I care for newly planted trees?

Water well right after planting so the root ball is fully soaked and the soil settles around it. After that, check the soil at the base twice a week. If the top few inches feel dry, water slowly and deeply so moisture reaches the roots. Deep watering builds stronger roots than quick surface watering.

In warm weather, trees usually want weekly watering. If it's cool and rainy for several days, you may not need to water at all. Keep the area free of weeds and fallen leaves, loosen the top layer once or twice a month, and don't pile mulch against the trunk in the first year.

How should I maintain my patio or stone features?

Joint material between stones can settle or wash out over time, especially where gaps are wider. If joints look low, refill them with matching sand or polymeric sand, following the product directions, to keep everything steady and neat.
