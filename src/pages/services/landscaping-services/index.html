Hardscape

Paver patios

A patio should feel like another room of the house: flat, solid, and good for decades. Ours start underground, with a properly excavated, compacted base and drainage built in, so it's still level after the spring thaw.

  • Excavation to firm subgrade, sloped away from the house
  • Geotextile fabric and a crushed base compacted in lifts
  • Pavers, large-format slabs or flagstone in the pattern you choose
  • Edge restraint and polymeric joint sand to lock it in
  • Matching walkways, steps and seat walls

Best forBackyard living space, walkways, hot tub pads and side yards.

Turf

Artificial turf & putting greens

Green from the first thaw to the first snow, with no mowing, no watering and no brown patches. A good turf install is mostly what's underneath: we excavate, build a compacted base that drains, then seam and secure the turf so it stays tight and level.

  • Backyards, front yards and narrow side strips
  • Custom putting greens with cups and flags
  • Compacted, permeable base so water drains through
  • Concrete curb or paver edging for a clean line
  • Low maintenance through every season

Best forShady spots where grass struggles, busy family yards, and anyone done with mowing.

Groundwork

Rough & final grading

Whether you're a homeowner or a homebuilder, we handle rough and final grading with certification guaranteed. Grading decides where water goes for the life of the house, so we set it to your lot grading plan and slope it away from the foundation.

  • Rough grade for homebuilders and developers
  • Final grade: topsoil placed and shaped to the approved plan
  • Positive drainage away from the foundation
  • Certification guaranteed: if our grading doesn't pass, we make it right
  • Ready for sod or a full landscape right after

Best forNew builds that need final grade approval, and any yard where water pools near the house.

Hardscape

Retaining walls

Retaining walls hold back soil, create usable levels and frame garden beds. Built right, they stay straight for decades. Built wrong, they lean within a few winters. We build on a compacted base with drainage behind the wall.

  • Segmental block and armour stone walls
  • Compacted base under the first course
  • Drainage aggregate behind the wall
  • Terraced beds and raised planters
  • Steps built into slopes

Best forSloped lots, walkout basements, and yards that need more flat space.
